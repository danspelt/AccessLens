## Tailored opening lines

Replace the second paragraph of the base email with these.

1. **VDRC** — "Organizations like yours are who this was built for. Your members are
   constantly weighing whether a place will actually work before they leave home — AccessLens
   tries to answer that with community-reported information, and I'd love the DRC community
   involved both as users and contributors."
2. **Neil Squire** — "You build assistive technology for exactly the people AccessLens
   serves. I've crossed paths with the Neil Squire team before, and I think there's a natural
   fit — both as a sponsor and as a community that could help the Victoria dataset grow."
3. **SCI BC** — "Your peer-support community makes the 'can I actually get in?' call
   constantly. AccessLens exists to answer it before they leave the house — built from
   community reports, not inspections."
4. **CanAssist** — "CanAssist builds technology that fills unmet accessibility needs, and
   that's exactly the gap AccessLens addresses on the information side: people can't tell
   whether a place is actually accessible until they arrive."
5. **Chamber** — "Your members lose customers they never knew they had when people can't
   confirm a venue is accessible ahead of time. AccessLens makes accessible businesses
   findable — and a Chamber partnership could put that in front of hundreds of Victoria
   businesses."
6. **Destination Greater Victoria** — "Victoria attracts visitors who plan entire trips
   around whether places are accessible — and today there's no reliable source for that.
   AccessLens is building it, starting here."
7. **City of Victoria** — "The City's accessibility framework sets the standard;
   AccessLens adds the missing piece on the public-information side — community-reported
   accessibility details for the places people actually visit."
8. **Saanich** — "Saanich's accessibility plan covers municipal services well;
   AccessLens complements it with community-reported accessibility information for the
   businesses and public places residents visit every day."
9. **United Way** — "This is a community-information project first and a business second —
   the goal is free, reliable accessibility information for Greater Victoria, and a small
   sponsorship is what gets the dataset growing."
10. **VREB** — "Realtors field accessibility questions constantly and rarely have good
    answers. AccessLens gives members a real resource for clients with mobility needs —
    and a sponsorship puts VREB's name on making that information public."
