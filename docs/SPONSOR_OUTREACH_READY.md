## 1. Victoria Disability Resource Centre

**To:** reception@drcvictoria.com
**Subject:** Supporting free accessibility information for Victoria

Hi there,

Organizations like yours are who this was built for. Your members are constantly weighing whether a place will actually work before they leave home — AccessLens tries to answer that with community-reported information, and I'd love the DRC community involved both as users and contributors.

I'm Dan Spelt, the developer behind AccessLens (https://www.accesslens.ca), a free, community-driven guide to accessibility information for public places in Victoria.

People can check reported features before a visit — level entrances, accessible washrooms, parking, elevators, and more — and read community experiences. As of September 26, 2026, the Victoria dataset has 48 active place records across restaurants, government buildings, parks, transit, libraries, and other categories. These are community-reported, not independent inspections or certifications.

I'm looking for a first local sponsor to help expand the Victoria dataset. A pilot could be small and clearly defined — for example, a $1,000 community sponsorship tied to a specific dataset-expansion goal, arranged by invoice. Sponsors receive acknowledgement, but they never influence which places are listed, what the checklists say, or how places score.

Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation in the next couple of weeks? The one-page prospectus is attached.

Thanks for considering it,

Dan Spelt
danspelt24@gmail.com
https://www.accesslens.ca
